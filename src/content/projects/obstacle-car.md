## Overview

A car that drives itself, detects obstacles ahead, stops and steers around them with no remote control.

- **Goal:** detect, stop and avoid without a human in the loop
- **Hardware:** 555-based timing and control logic, ultrasonic distance sensors, an L293D motor driver, DC motors and a chassis
- **Outcome:** a working obstacle-avoiding robot, and a grounding in sensor integration and motor control
