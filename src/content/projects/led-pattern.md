## Overview

A breadboarded circuit where an Arduino drives a row of LEDs through programmed sequences.

- **Goal:** striking light patterns from simple parts and basic timing logic
- **Hardware:** Arduino, LEDs, current-limiting resistors and breadboard wiring
- **Outcome:** working sequences, and practice with digital outputs and simple embedded programs
