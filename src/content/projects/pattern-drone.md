## Overview

For ECE 2544, I programmed a drone to fly a path that spells out the course number. The flight was scripted, then flown autonomously, with GPS and IMU feedback holding the shape and a remote control kept ready for manual override.

## What it does

- **Pattern flight.** Traces "ECE 2544" as a continuous scripted path.
- **Autonomous navigation.** Follows the programmed route without pilot input.
- **Precision.** GPS and IMU fusion keep the letters legible.
- **Reusable.** The pattern can be swapped for any other path.

## Outcome

The drone flew the full pattern, a practical lesson in flight scripting and in autonomous path following.
