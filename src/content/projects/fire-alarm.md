## Overview

A simple, low-cost alarm. A thermistor senses heat and an IC555 timer circuit drives a buzzer once the threshold is crossed.

- **Goal:** a cheap alarm that reacts to a rise in temperature
- **Hardware:** IC555 timer, thermistor, buzzer, resistors and capacitors, small DC supply
- **Outcome:** a reliable prototype, and my introduction to timer circuits and sensor interfacing
