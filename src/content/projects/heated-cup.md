## Overview

A copper heating coil wrapped around a steel cup, switched by an Arduino reading a temperature sensor. When the drink cools below a threshold the coil heats it back up.

- **Goal:** keep a drink at a comfortable temperature with simple, safe control
- **Hardware:** Arduino, temperature sensor, copper heating coil, steel cup, low-voltage supply
- **Outcome:** a working prototype, and a first taste of closed-loop temperature control
